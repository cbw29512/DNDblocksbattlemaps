Starter maps

Open the map builder. Under Create a Starter Map, choose Roadside Inn, Castle Keep, Starter Dungeon, Forest Camp, Harbor Dock, Goblin Cave, Ancient Temple, or Ruined Outpost. Choose Create New Map. Each choice creates a separate editable copy. Existing maps are not overwritten. Use the Maps list to switch between saved maps.

Build Mode

Choose Build, Props, Characters, or Monsters and click the grid to place blocks. All geometry uses 5-foot cube units. Larger monsters combine many cubes into one creature. Use the camera buttons or mouse to rotate and zoom. Right-click removes a placed block; Undo/Redo reverse edits.

Catalog order: On an Inn map, Inn and furniture blocks appear first alphabetically, followed by the rest alphabetically. Other maps similarly prioritize relevant blocks. Nothing is hidden; search still covers everything.

Combat Mode

Use the Combat Mode control. Building tools are hidden and scenery editing is locked. Creature Markers and Spells & Areas appear only in Combat Mode. Select a character or monster, then click its destination square. Press Esc to cancel. The DM can move creatures in the current local prototype.

Identity and status rings

In Combat Mode, open Creature Markers and drag a non-red identity ring onto a Character. Red belongs to Monsters. To use a condition ring, click the status, then click a character or monster to apply or remove it. The selected status stays active so you can mark several creatures. Click the status again or press Esc to clear your selection. Drag-and-drop status rings also works. Exhaustion advances through six levels before clearing. Statuses are visual reminders and do not automatically execute D&D rules.

Campaign Party

In Build Mode, place a Character and check Party. It appears across the existing maps and any new starter maps with the same ID, identity ring and statuses. Each map remembers its own position. A template's party entrance provides initial placement.

Backup your maps

Click Backup Maps in the builder to download a versioned JSON file containing the current browser's DND Blocks saves. Keep that file somewhere safe. This is read-only: it does not move, alter or delete your maps. Restoring/importing backups is not yet available.

Print and save

The app automatically saves maps locally in this browser. Print Map generates a printable battle map. Saving maps to other devices and live player sync are not implemented yet.

Area spell preview (prototype)

Choose an effect under Spell & Area Preview and select Preview Area. Move over the battlefield to preview 5-foot translucent cubes. Left-click or tap a square to cast and write one announcement in the Combat Log; alternatively press Cast after positioning. Escape, right-click or the touch-friendly Cancel button dismisses without casting. The preview currently uses provisional center-cell geometry and the first creature as caster origin: this is for visualization, not certified rules adjudication. Saving throws, affected-creature calculations and persistent combat logs are not yet implemented.

Spell preview target counts

The spell preview can list creatures whose occupied cubes intersect its highlighted cubes. Large, Huge, and Gargantuan creatures count once. These are provisional preview candidates, not verified D&D RAW targeting, saving throws, or automatic damage. Spells do not yet resolve attacks.

Candidate targets in area previews

Creatures whose 5-foot cubes intersect the provisional spell area receive a yellow highlight, including all occupied cells of Large, Huge, and Gargantuan creatures. Escape, right-click, or Cancel clears the preview without casting. The highlight identifies candidates only: saving throws, cover, line of effect, and exact 2014/2024 rules are not yet certified.

Choose the spell caster

Before using Preview Area, choose the character or monster on the map who is casting. The preview uses that creature as its origin for range and direction. No creature selected means the preview will not start; the map no longer guesses the first creature.

How spell shapes fit our cube grid

Important: Area spells are displayed using DND Blocks' square, 5-foot grid. Even when a D&D spell describes a circular radius, sphere, or cone, its area is converted to complete highlighted 5-foot squares/cubes for our battle map. This changes the visual outline to a stepped, block-based shape; it does not change the spell's listed range, radius, saving throw, damage, or other rules. Which full cubes count as affected is determined by the applicable edition's grid rules, not by guessing or applying partial damage. Exact 3D geometry and line of effect remain under certification.

Fireball grid-square preview

Fireball's circular horizontal footprint now uses the tabletop half-square grid rule rather than simply testing square centers: only squares at least halfway covered light up, with each affected space displayed as a complete translucent 5-foot cube. For a 20-foot radius positioned on a grid intersection, the horizontal footprint is 52 squares, not a filled 8-by-8 box. Vertical geometry, walls, line of effect, and exact creature adjudication are still under review; this remains a targeting preview, not automatic RAW damage.

Which spell measurements are used?

Each spell, weapon and monster ability must use its own checked measurements for its specific rules edition (2014 or 2024) and source creature. A radius, cone or line can differ between editions or monster variants, so DND Blocks must not guess or substitute a similarly named action. The shape is displayed with full 5-foot cubes, but the original listed measurements, saves and damage are preserved. Features whose values or targeting rules are not yet verified are marked as previews, not confirmed RAW hits.

Edition-specific spell previews

The spell chooser now labels examples by edition (2014 or 2024). Entries marked sample preview — unverified are illustrative cube shapes and should not be relied on as confirmed rules measurements or automatic spell resolutions. Verified edition-specific measurements and individual monster breath abilities will be added separately.

Line-shaped spell previews

Horizontal, grid-aligned line effects are shown as a movable row of complete 5-foot cubes, one square wide and approximately 50% transparent. A 60-foot example uses 12 cubes; a 100-foot example uses 20 cubes. The actual length comes from the selected, edition-specific ability, not from the color or name. This early preview snaps direction to the nearest cardinal grid axis; diagonal lines, wall blocking, and full RAW line-of-effect decisions are still under development.

Self-range spell starting points

For a spell or monster ability whose area originates from Self, such as a line or cone beginning at its caster, the effect begins at the selected character or monster. Pointing or dragging the preview changes the direction, not the starting square. Point-targeted areas such as Fireball can be positioned independently within their permitted range. Exact origin choices for larger creatures and complete 2014/2024 RAW grid behavior are still being certified.

Large creatures casting Self effects

For a Self-starting horizontal line or cone from a Large, Huge or Gargantuan creature, the provisional preview begins at the creature's front occupied cube in the aimed cardinal direction, not the back anchor square. The creature remains one grouped cube model. Diagonal and vertical origins still require RAW and browser verification.

Diagonal line aiming (preview)

Line previews can now point along eight grid directions while retaining full 5-foot translucent cubes. Diagonal square-to-square travel is longer than horizontal travel, so a 60-foot line preview shows fewer diagonal cells than horizontal cells. This is a visual aiming preview, not yet a certified RAW list of affected squares; walls, diagonal partial-square rules and 3D line effects still require review.
